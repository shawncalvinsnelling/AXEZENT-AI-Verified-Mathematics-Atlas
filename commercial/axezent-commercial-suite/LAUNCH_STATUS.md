## Paid activation status

General public checkout is **not enabled from this repository**.

Before general paid software activation, AXEZENT must operationally confirm the applicable payment-provider business-account requirements and the required sales-tax or other tax registration/taxability treatment for the transaction.

Until those operational items are confirmed for a specific order:

- customers must not send unsolicited payments;
- posted prices remain Preview pricing;
- no automatic recurring billing is used; and
- paid access is activated only through a written, manually confirmed order where legally and operationally ready.

## Not represented as deployed

The repository does not represent the following as currently complete unless separate production evidence establishes it:

- automated Cash App merchant/API payment verification;
- a production entitlement backend for every Preview product;
- automated customer account provisioning; or
- general public recurring billing.
