# AXEZENT Corporate Operating Standard

## Organization

- **Parent brand:** AXEZENT
- **Research/technology brand:** AXEZENT AI
- **Commercial division:** AXEZENT Commercial Software Suite
- **Customer payment destination:** Cash App **$axezent**
- **A-Z portfolio:** 26 product ventures listed in VENTURES.md

The current public portfolio does not by itself create 26 separate corporations, LLCs, bank accounts, trademarks, or tax registrations.

## Decision standard

Operate with corporate, legal, scientific, and audit discipline:

1. **Truth first.** Distinguish proved, verified-bounded, experimental, hypothesis, prototype, and unverified claims.
2. **IP first.** Do not publish enabling private invention detail before an intentional IP decision.
3. **Contracts first.** Enterprise rights arise from written terms, not marketing language.
4. **Evidence first.** Revenue, customer validation, patent filing, scientific validation, and performance claims require records.
5. **Least disclosure.** Public materials disclose what customers need to evaluate/use the product, not the private engine.
6. **Server-side enforcement.** Paid capabilities are authorized by private infrastructure.
7. **Non-destructive enforcement.** Nonpayment disables premium service; it never damages or deletes customer property.
8. **Separation of namespaces.** Research proof, commercial claims, patent claims, and marketing claims are maintained separately.
9. **Version control.** Material releases receive dated versions, hashes, and changelogs.
10. **Human approval for legal acts.** Incorporation, patent filings, signed contracts, banking, taxes, and regulatory representations require authorized human action.

## Portfolio continuity

AXEZENT's prior business, patent, invention, research, and software work is treated as a continuing portfolio. Historical names or concepts should not be silently promoted to filed-patent, validated-product, or production status. Their latest evidence-backed status controls.
