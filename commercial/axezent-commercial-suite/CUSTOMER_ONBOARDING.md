# Customer Onboarding

## 1. Select access

Choose an AXEZENT plan or individual product from [Pricing](./PRICING.md).

## 2. Request activation

For research, licensing, or enterprise onboarding, contact **Axezentai@gmail.com** with:

- name and organization, if applicable;
- requested product or plan;
- intended research/workflow;
- number of users;
- whether API or private deployment is required.

Do not email passwords, private keys, payment PINs, or confidential research files during initial onboarding.

## 3. Payment

The current customer-facing payment destination is **Cash App $axezent**. Payment must be independently confirmed by AXEZENT before an entitlement is activated.

## 4. Entitlement

Once confirmed, AXEZENT issues or extends access for the applicable product/plan. Premium services verify the entitlement before running paid operations.

## 5. Technical onboarding

Customers receive the relevant client instructions, API documentation, limits, and support channel for activated products.

## Product availability

The public **Six-Cycle Exact Certifier** is available for evaluation now. Other products are introduced by release stage and are not billed until activated.
