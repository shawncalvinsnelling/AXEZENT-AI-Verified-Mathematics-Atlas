# Customer Onboarding

## 1. Select a product or plan

Review the [Preview Launch Pricing](./PRICING.md) and [Launch Status](./LAUNCH_STATUS.md).

A Preview listing is not a guarantee that the product is currently available for paid activation.

## 2. Request activation

For research, licensing, or enterprise onboarding, contact **Axezentai@gmail.com** with:

- name and organization, if applicable;
- requested product or plan;
- intended research or workflow;
- number of users; and
- whether API or private deployment is requested.

Do not email passwords, private keys, payment PINs, full payment-card numbers, or confidential research files during initial onboarding.

## 3. Receive written activation terms

Before payment, AXEZENT will confirm in writing:

- the product or service being activated;
- whether it is Available or Preview;
- price and access period;
- usage limits or scope, if applicable;
- applicable taxes or tax treatment;
- approved payment instructions;
- the [Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md);
- the [Refund and Cancellation Policy](./REFUND_CANCELLATION_POLICY.md); and
- the [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

The customer must affirmatively accept the applicable order and terms before paid activation.

## 4. Payment

Do not send unsolicited payment. Use only the payment instructions supplied for the specific order.

## 5. Entitlement

After payment is independently confirmed, AXEZENT issues or extends access for the applicable product and period. Activated premium services use the applicable entitlement process before running paid operations.

## 6. Technical onboarding

Customers receive the relevant client instructions, API documentation, stated limits, support channel, and current product-status information.

## Product availability

The public **Six-Cycle Exact Certifier** is available for evaluation now. The broader product portfolio remains in Preview or controlled development unless the Launch Status or a written activation explicitly says otherwise.
