# AXEZENT Enterprise

AXEZENT Enterprise is the contracting path for organization-level mathematical verification, formalization, research automation, private discovery services, OEM arrangements, and controlled deployments.

**AXEZENT is an independent research and software brand operated by Shawn Calvin Snelling.**

## Enterprise capabilities that may be scoped by agreement

- organization and team licensing;
- higher API or verification limits where supported;
- private or controlled deployment options where supported;
- reproducibility and audit receipts;
- priority technical support;
- custom integrations;
- OEM and field-of-use licensing; and
- negotiated confidentiality and data-handling terms.

A listed capability is not represented as generally available unless a written proposal or agreement confirms it for the customer.

## Deployment

Enterprise deployments are scoped according to selected products, readiness, compute requirements, user count, integration needs, security requirements, and the written agreement.

Private discovery services remain server-side unless a separate written license expressly provides otherwise.

## Security and authorization

For activated premium services, authorization is enforced through the applicable AXEZENT entitlement process. The public repository contains reference entitlement components but is not itself a representation that every production backend is deployed.

Customer credentials and financial secrets must not be stored in the public repository. Licensing telemetry is not designed to contain customer proofs, source code, prompts, or unpublished research.

## Intellectual property

AXEZENT retains rights in pre-existing software, algorithms, infrastructure, research systems, and other background technology except where a signed agreement expressly states otherwise.

Customer-supplied materials remain customer content. Project-specific ownership, work-product rights, confidentiality, and permitted use are governed by the applicable written agreement.

## Commercial terms

An enterprise agreement may address:

- term and renewal;
- named or metered users;
- API and compute allocations;
- service scope and acceptance;
- support commitments and service levels;
- confidentiality;
- security and data handling;
- deployment scope;
- ownership and license rights;
- field-of-use restrictions where lawful and applicable;
- taxes, invoicing, and payment;
- warranties and liability;
- suspension and termination; and
- dispute terms.

Enterprise pricing is **quoted from $25,000 per year** and becomes binding only in an executed order or agreement.
