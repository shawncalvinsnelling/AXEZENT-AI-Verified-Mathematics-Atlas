# AXEZENT Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Effective date:** September 23, 2026

## Current billing model

AXEZENT's current manual paid-access process is **non-automatic**. A price described as monthly represents a 30-day access period unless a written order says otherwise.

AXEZENT does not automatically charge a customer for another period under the current manual process. A customer who does not want another period can simply decline to purchase or authorize a renewal.

## Before activation

If AXEZENT receives an authorized payment but cannot activate the purchased service, the customer may request a refund of the unactivated payment.

A duplicate or mistaken overpayment should be reported promptly and will be reviewed for correction or refund.

## After activation

After paid access has been activated, payments are generally non-refundable for change of mind, except where:

- applicable law requires a refund;
- AXEZENT approves a refund because of duplicate billing or an incorrect charge;
- AXEZENT cannot provide the material paid service that was confirmed in the applicable order; or
- a written order or enterprise agreement provides a different refund right.

Nothing in this policy limits a non-waivable consumer right.

## Cancellation

Because current manual access does not automatically renew, cancellation means declining the next access period. Unless a refund or written agreement states otherwise, existing access continues through the already-paid period.

If automatic recurring billing is introduced in the future, AXEZENT will disclose the recurring terms and cancellation method before enrollment and obtain express consent before recurring charges begin.

## How to request help

Email **Axezentai@gmail.com** with:

- the customer's name;
- product or service;
- payment date;
- amount;
- transaction or receipt identifier, if available; and
- the reason for the request.

Do **not** send passwords, Cash App PINs, full payment-card numbers, private keys, or other financial credentials.

Approved refunds are returned through an appropriate payment method and may be subject to the payment provider's processing time.
