# AXEZENT Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 23, 2026

AXEZENT is an independent research and software brand operated by Shawn Calvin Snelling. This Privacy Policy describes information AXEZENT may process when a person requests activation, uses an activated service, contacts support, or uses licensing infrastructure.

## 1. Information AXEZENT may process

Depending on the service and the information a customer chooses to provide, AXEZENT may process:

- name and contact information;
- organization name and role, when provided;
- requested product, plan, user count, and service preferences;
- payment-confirmation information and transaction metadata needed to verify an order;
- support communications and troubleshooting information;
- entitlement information such as product ID, license or account token, installation ID, application version, request timestamp, expiration, and revocation status;
- security and abuse-prevention information reasonably necessary to protect the service; and
- customer content only when the customer intentionally submits it to a service or support channel.

AXEZENT does not need a customer's Cash App password, payment PIN, private financial credentials, private keys, or account password to activate a service. Customers should never send those items.

## 2. Licensing telemetry boundary

The entitlement client is designed to transmit only licensing and access-control information such as:

- product ID;
- license key or account token;
- installation ID;
- application version; and
- request timestamp.

The entitlement client is not designed to transmit customer proofs, source code, research files, prompts, unpublished mathematics, or other research content as payment or licensing telemetry.

## 3. Purposes of processing

AXEZENT may use information to:

- respond to inquiries and provide quotes;
- verify orders and payments;
- activate, authenticate, meter, expire, or revoke service access;
- provide requested software, APIs, support, and enterprise services;
- secure services and investigate abuse or technical failures;
- maintain business, tax, accounting, transaction, and compliance records;
- improve public or paid products using information that can lawfully be used for that purpose; and
- comply with legal obligations and enforce agreements.

AXEZENT will not intentionally use customer content for an unrelated purpose without authorization or another lawful basis.

## 4. Sharing

AXEZENT may disclose information only as reasonably necessary to:

- payment providers that process or verify a customer payment;
- hosting, infrastructure, email, security, or software providers used to operate the service;
- professional advisers or contractors subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations;
- authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, users, or service security; or
- a successor in connection with a lawful reorganization, sale, or transfer of the business, subject to applicable law.

AXEZENT does **not** sell customer personal data for money and does not use entitlement telemetry to sell customer research content.

## 5. Customer research content

Customer-supplied proofs, source code, prompts, research files, and unpublished mathematics remain customer content. AXEZENT processes such content only when intentionally submitted for the requested service or support purpose.

Customers should not send confidential research through public GitHub issues.

## 6. Retention

AXEZENT retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including providing access, support, fraud prevention, security, recordkeeping, tax and accounting requirements, dispute resolution, and legal obligations.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary and no legal retention requirement applies, AXEZENT will delete it or render it non-identifying where reasonably practicable.

## 7. Security

AXEZENT uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the current service, including keeping production signing secrets and financial credentials out of the public repository.

No Internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Privacy requests

A person may contact **Axezentai@gmail.com** with the subject line **Privacy Request** to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of personal information, or to ask about processing.

An activated customer may also use the private support or activation channel supplied with that customer's service.

AXEZENT may request information reasonably necessary to authenticate a request. Where applicable law provides additional privacy rights, appeal rights, response deadlines, or opt-out rights, AXEZENT will honor those rights to the extent they apply.

AXEZENT does not require a person to disclose account passwords or payment PINs to make a privacy request.

## 9. Children

AXEZENT paid services are not directed to children under 13 and AXEZENT does not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13 through the paid activation process.

## 10. Third-party services

Links to GitHub, Zenodo, ORCID, Cash App, journals, or other third-party services are governed by those services' own privacy policies and terms.

## 11. Changes

AXEZENT may update this Privacy Policy as services change or legal requirements develop. The effective date above will be updated when a material revision is published.

## 12. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to **Axezentai@gmail.com**.
