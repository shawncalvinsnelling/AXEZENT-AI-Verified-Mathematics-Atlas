# AXEZENT Product Claims and Research Status Notice

AXEZENT uses evidence-scoped terminology.

## What "exact" means

When an AXEZENT tool describes a calculation or check as **exact**, it means the identified operation is performed with the stated symbolic, integer, rational, formal, or otherwise non-approximate method within the implemented scope. It does not mean that every mathematical statement outside that scope has been proven.

## What "verified" or "certificate" means

A verification receipt or certificate records the stated checks for the stated inputs, assumptions, formulas, software version, and scope.

It is not automatically:

- independent peer review;
- journal acceptance;
- approval by a university, government, standards body, or professional society;
- professional accreditation;
- a patentability or freedom-to-operate opinion;
- an exhaustive literature search;
- a guarantee of historical priority; or
- a guarantee that a broader conjecture or open problem is solved.

## Literature and novelty tools

A literature-comparison or novelty tool reports the sources and searches actually performed. Absence of a match is not a guarantee that no prior art, publication, preprint, thesis, conference paper, patent, or other disclosure exists.

## Manuscript auditing

A Journal Proof Auditor or similar product performs the stated reproducibility, formula-consistency, boundary-case, and certificate checks. It is independent software and is not a journal, editorial board, referee, or guarantee of publication.

## Open problems

AXEZENT distinguishes exact verified artifacts from open mathematical problems. A product name or research workflow does not change an open problem's status.

## Third-party references

References to Adin, Hegedüs, Roichman, journals, GitHub, Zenodo, ORCID, computer-algebra systems, universities, or other third parties describe source material, interoperability, or research context only. No sponsorship, endorsement, certification, partnership, or affiliation is implied unless expressly stated in writing.

## Product availability

A product labeled **Preview** is under development or controlled release. Preview pricing does not represent general availability and a Preview product is not billed unless AXEZENT separately confirms an activation.
