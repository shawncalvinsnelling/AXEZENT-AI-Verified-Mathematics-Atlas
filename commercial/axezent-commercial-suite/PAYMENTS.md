# Billing & Activation

## No unsolicited payments

**Do not send payment solely because a price or payment identifier appears in an older document, message, screenshot, or repository history.**

AXEZENT provides current payment instructions only after confirming a specific product or service, availability, price, access period, applicable taxes or tax treatment, and the governing terms.

A payment sent without written activation instructions does not by itself create an entitlement.

## Current billing model

The current customer billing process is manual and non-automatic.

- A price described as monthly means a 30-day access period unless a written order says otherwise.
- Current manual access does not automatically renew.
- AXEZENT does not automatically charge the customer for another period under the current manual process.
- A future recurring-billing program, if offered, will require a separate enrollment and express consent to the recurring terms before recurring charges begin.

## Approved payment channels

AXEZENT may use Cash App or another payment provider for an approved order.

If Cash App is used for the sale of goods or services, AXEZENT will use it only through an account configuration permitted for business sales and subject to the provider's current terms. Customers should rely on the payment instructions supplied for their specific activation.

## Tax readiness

Software and related services can be subject to sales, use, or similar taxes depending on the product and jurisdiction. AXEZENT confirms applicable tax handling before a general paid activation and may add or collect tax when required by law.

## Activation

After an authorized payment is confirmed, AXEZENT may issue or extend the customer's entitlement for the stated product and access period.

A screenshot or unverified payment claim is not sufficient for activation.

## Receipts

Customers should retain the payment-provider receipt and the AXEZENT activation or order confirmation.

## Renewals

When a paid access period ends, the entitlement expires unless the customer purchases or authorizes another period. Expiration does not delete customer-owned files or alter customer-owned data on the customer's device.

## Refunds and cancellation

See the [Refund and Cancellation Policy](./REFUND_CANCELLATION_POLICY.md).

## Security

AXEZENT never needs a customer's Cash App password, PIN, full payment-card number, private key, or private financial credential in GitHub, software configuration files, or support messages.
